================================================================================
MANAGERIAL IMPLICATIONS: OPERATIONAL USE OF CANCELLATION PREDICTIONS
================================================================================
1. Practical Prediction Timing in Hotel Operations:
   - Initial Booking (Strict Model): 100-30 days out. Hotel uses AUC = 0.7372 baseline to flag
     high-risk segments (wholesale groups, extended lead times) for deposit terms.
   - Dynamic Pre-Arrival (Extended Model): 14-3 days out. Incorporating guest engagement (special requests,
     booking changes) sharpens discrimination to AUC = 0.7953 for final arrival readiness.
2. Insufficiency of Accuracy in Imbalanced Settings:
   - In this 2017 test set (38.8% cancellation rate), predicting 'never cancel' yields 61.2% accuracy
     with ZERO detection (Recall = 0%). Precision (71.5%) and Recall (54.7%) govern revenue trade-offs.
3. Caution Against Premature 'Optimal' Overbooking Policies:
   - Mathematical optimization via the critical fractile Cu / (Cu + Co) is IMPOSSIBLE without:
     * Underage Cost (Cu): Lost room margin (ADR minus marginal servicing cost).
     * Overage Cost (Co): Penalty of 'walking' a booked guest (rebooking, transport, goodwill loss).
   - Because Co is typically multiple times Cu, risk scores should guide communication and deposit
     rules rather than unconstrained capacity overbooking.
